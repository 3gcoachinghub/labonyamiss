Curriculum Architecture

Core Pillars of O & A Level Chemistry

01

Physical Chemistry

Atomic structure, chemical bonding, mole concept, energetics, rate equations, dynamic equilibria, electrochemistry, and thermodynamics.

02

Inorganic Chemistry & Periodicity

Periodic trends, Group 2 alkaline earths, Group 17 halogens, transition metals, ligand exchange, complex ions, and qualitative salt analysis.

03

Organic Chemistry & Mechanisms

Reaction mechanisms (electrophilic/nucleophilic), curly-arrow accuracy, functional group interconversions, aromatic chemistry, and synthetic routes.

04

Practical & ATP Lab Skills

Comprehensive prep for CAIE Paper 3 & Paper 5, and Alternative to Practical (ATP) papers: titration techniques, thermometric curves, and ion identification.

05

Spectroscopy & Analysis

Infrared (IR) spectroscopy, Mass spectrometry fragmentation, Proton (1H) and Carbon-13 (13C) NMR structural elucidation.

06

Mark Scheme Technique

Aligning answers precisely with CAIE & Edexcel mark schemes, mastering structured calculation steps, and avoiding common examiner traps.
